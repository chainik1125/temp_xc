## Hypothesis (honest negative)

On the Wang procedure for emergent misalignment (Turner et al. 2025) on
the Qwen-14B-Instruct + financial-advice organism, the per-token SAE
arditi baseline beats both TXC-base and TXC-pro on the single-feature
champion metric. The arch gap *widens* in the harder R32 organism
regime where the dictionary has to "do real work" (gap rises from
~+4 to +12.58 align points).

This is a negative TXC result and the paper reports it as such.

## Salvageable contribution

The interesting cross-arch finding from Dmitry's work: **the bundle
null result is architecture-general**. Both SAE arditi and TXC at k=30
peak at align ≈ 41.3, falling 13–23 align points below their
respective single-feat champions, despite a 3× difference in bundle-vector
norm. The "distributed misalignment that can be reassembled by summing
features" hypothesis is falsified in *both* dictionaries.

This is interpretable contribution even though TXC loses on the
probing-style headline.

## Setup

- **Subject model**: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` + LoRA finance organism
  (`ModelOrganismsForEM/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct_R1_0_1_0_finance_extended_train`).
- **Hookpoint**: layer 24 `resid_post`. d_model=5120.
- **Architectures**: SAE arditi (TopK k=128, d_sae=32768), TXC-base
  (TopK k=128, d_sae=32768), TXC-pro (TopK k=20 per pos, d_sae=32768).
- **Wang procedure** (4 stages):
  1. Δz̄ encoder rank
  2. causal screen at α = ±1
  3. strength sweep
  4. final per-feat α frontier
- **Eval prompts**: 8 Betley first-person EM prompts × 8 rollouts = 64
  per cell. Gemini judge.
- **Cells**: arch × steps × organism × α-regime ∈ {R1 mid-α, R32 ext-α} = 8.

## Caveats

- 14B model + LoRA at fp16 ≈ 28 GB. H100 (80GB) is comfortable.
  H200 reserved for fallback.
- Gemini judge variance: σ ≈ 6 align points at n=64. Differences below
  this are noise.
