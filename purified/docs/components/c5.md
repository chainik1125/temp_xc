## Hypothesis (revised, modest)

On the RLHF-sentiment steering case study from the T-SAE paper § 4.4,
TXC-base and TXC-pro produce coherence-vs-success curves that are
comparable to T-SAE; both architectures match T-SAE at high coherence
(coh ≥ 1.75). This is a "matches the baseline" result, not a "beats"
result.

We deliberately do **not** chase the steering wins from Y/W's
hill-climbing (Galaxy 8/11/18, SoftMaxPool, ContrastiveMergeH8): those
architectures lose 0.005–0.02 probing AUC vs the canonical leaderboard
(see `2026-05-02-yw-T8-benchmark.md`), so they're inconsistent with the
"two TXCs everywhere" methodology. The paper accepts a softer C5 claim
in exchange for the harder C3+C4 claims.

## Setup

- **Subject model**: same as C4 — Gemma-2-2b-IT layer 13.
- **Architectures**: T-SAE (paper config), TXC-base, TXC-pro. Three total.
- **Steering protocol**: per-token decoder-row addition at the residual
  stream. For window archs, V7 tiled-broadcast (stride-T blocks, single
  uniform δ within each block) — chosen for arch-uniformity, not for
  peak performance on any single arch.
- **Coherence thresholds**: {1.5, 1.75, 2.0, 2.25, 2.5}.
- **Metric**: success rate within each coh ≥ τ band, 3-seed mean ± stderr.
- **Seeds**: {1, 2, 42}.
- **Hardware**: 1× A40 sufficient.

## Caveats

- The "winning" steering archs from hill-climbing are excluded by design.
  Footnote in the paper acknowledging this explicitly.
- V7 tiled-broadcast loses for end-position-discriminative encoders
  (e.g. H8 contrastive at T=2). TXC-pro uses subseq encoder + multi-distance
  contrastive; pre-test that V7 is OK for TXC-pro, otherwise switch to PP.
