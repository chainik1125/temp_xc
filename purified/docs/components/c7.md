## Hypothesis

On the Ward et al. 2025 backtracking case study (Stage B), TXC delivers
the largest peak Δgc (gain in Sonnet-judged genuine backtracking count
under steering), roughly 3× the next-best architecture. This is the
"TXC pushes the Pareto frontier on a behavioural inducement metric"
claim.

Detection of backtracking sentences via sparse linear probes on TXC
features is competitive with conventional SAEs but **not** dominant
once the class-imbalance-aware metric (PR-AUC) is used; Wilcoxon vs
each baseline at S=8 fails Holm-Bonferroni (smallest p_holm = 0.31).
The defensible paper claim on detection is "comparable" rather than
"SOTA".

## Setup

- **Subject model**: `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B` (BASE) at residual
  layer 10 (`resid_L10`). Backtracking is a base-model behaviour;
  Stage A traces are generated by `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B`
  reasoning continuations of the same model. Note: this diverges from
  the C3/C4/C5 anchor (`gemma-2-2b-it` L13) and matches Aniket's
  Stage B pipeline.
- **Activation cache**: ~24K FineWeb sequences × 128 tokens,
  fp16. Cached at three hookpoints (`resid_L10`, `attn_L10`,
  `ln1_L10`); the headline uses `resid_L10` for TXC and TFA, `ln1_L10`
  for the TopK-SAE (per Aniket's per-arch hookpoint selection on
  `rank_global_sonnet`).
- **Architectures (headline, 5)**: TopK-SAE (k=64/token), TXC (locked
  TXC-base, k=16/pos, T=6, window L0=96), TSAE-paper (Han attention
  TSAE w/ `kval_topk=20`), TFA (TSAE-paper + sinusoidal positional
  encodings), MLC (multi-layer crosscoder, T=5 stacked layers
  L8..L12).
- **Architectures (appendix, +1)**: TXC-H8 — locked TXC-pro w/ k=16/pos.
- **Cohort**: 31 truly-wrong + 30 originally-correct = 61 MATH-500
  questions × 25 magnitudes × 6 arches = 9 150 transcripts in
  `flip_matrix.parquet`.
- **Magnitude grid**: 25 points, densified ±0.5–8 around the SAE peak:
  `[-16,-12,-10,-8,-7,-6,-5,-4,-3,-2,-1,-0.5, 0, 0.5,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,10,12,16]`.
- **Cut protocol**: cut Stage A trace at 25% of unsteered length, then
  steer-and-continue (Aniket's B3 cut25 protocol — wins over LLM-judged
  cut and full-trace variants).
- **Two metric axes**:
  - **Inducement (Δgc)**: Sonnet 4.6 judge counts genuine backtracking
    events (catching errors, missing constraints, approach rejection,
    assumption re-evaluation), explicitly excluding filler /
    pseudo-bt / loops. Per-(arch, qid) baselined to mag=0.
  - **Detection (PR-AUC)**: sparse linear probes
    (`LogisticRegression(solver=liblinear, penalty=l1, C=1.0)`)
    fitted on top-S mean-difference features, S ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32},
    5-fold GroupKFold by `question_id`. **Primary metric is PR-AUC**
    (12 % positive class — F1 / ROC-AUC are misleading at this
    imbalance).
- **Seeds**: 42 only on this push; multi-seed work is reserved for the
  C7 reproduction in `temp_bench`.
- **Hardware**: 1× A40, ~3 hr per full sweep on 2-GPU parallelism.

## Results

### Inducement — Sonnet `genuine_count` (gold standard, 9 150 calls)

Stability column = number of nonzero magnitudes (out of 24) where
Δgc > 0.

| Arch | Stability (Δgc>0 / 24) | Peak Δgc (mag) | Peak / TXC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TXC** | 21/24 | **+1.574 (mag=−12)** | 1.00× |
| MLC | **24/24** | +0.508 (mag=+16) | 0.32× |
| TXC-H8 | 22/24 | +0.492 (mag=+12) | 0.31× |
| TFA | **24/24** | +0.328 (mag=+12) | 0.21× |
| SAE | 23/24 | +0.262 (mag=−16) | 0.17× |
| TSAE-paper | 23/24 | +0.246 (mag=+10) | 0.16× |

**Headline**: TXC peak inducement is ~3× the next-best architecture
even after Sonnet filters out filler / pseudo-backtracking / loops.
Trade-off: MLC and TFA achieve perfect 24/24 stability but with
3–5× lower peak. The 3 magnitudes where TXC's Δgc dips ≤ 0 are all on
the positive side (mag ∈ {+8, +10, +16}) — the same region where TXC
exhibits sentence-loop catastrophic regression (judge-free repetition
rate 0.55 at TXC mag=+16).

### Cheap proxy — Δ keyword_rate (`wait` / `hmm` per total words)

TXC dominates by an even larger margin under the unfiltered proxy
(peak ~14× MLC, ~30× the conventional SAEs), with all archs except
SAE monotonically positive across all 24 nonzero magnitudes. The
Sonnet-filtered gap is the more conservative reading; report Sonnet
in headline, keyword_rate in appendix.

### Rescue-correctness (initial framing — REJECTED after baseline correction)

The original "+6 to +8 net rescues" numbers are dominated by the
mag=0 resampling noise floor (cut-and-continue from the 25 % prefix
independently rescues 7 of 31 truly-wrong + regresses 1 of 30
originally-correct for **all six architectures**, identical
question_ids). Baseline-corrected `Δnet = extra_rescue − broke`:
no architecture has a statistically significant steering effect on
rescue-correctness (paired McNemar p ≥ 0.73 for every arch at every
peak magnitude).

This is why we pivoted the headline to inducement (Δgc) per Dmitry's
2026-05-03 PM Slack feedback: getting answers right is good but not
necessarily the best metric — what we can defend is "more inducement,
more stably across magnitudes".

### Detection — PR-AUC (12 % positive class)

| Arch | S=1 | S=2 | S=4 | S=8 | S=16 | S=32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TXC** | 0.188 | 0.211 | 0.234 | 0.243 | 0.252 | 0.279 |
| **TXC-H8** | 0.180 | 0.197 | 0.209 | 0.234 | 0.249 | **0.288** |
| **SAE** | 0.176 | 0.174 | 0.187 | 0.201 | 0.214 | 0.278 |
| **TSAE-paper** | 0.171 | 0.176 | 0.183 | 0.198 | 0.220 | 0.245 |
| **TFA** | 0.161 | 0.172 | 0.188 | 0.194 | 0.193 | 0.197 |
| **MLC** | 0.207 | 0.240 | 0.252 | **0.251** | 0.251 | 0.251 |

Random baseline at 12 % positive = 0.12; every arch comfortably beats
random. MLC leads at small S; TXC-H8 takes the top spot at S=32. No
Wilcoxon TXC-vs-baseline comparison at S=8 is HB-significant
(smallest p_holm = 0.31). Defensible paper claim: **"temporal-aware
architectures don't lose detection power vs conventional SAEs"** —
not "TXC is SOTA at detection".

### Hygiene

All 6 archs trained to FVU within 2× of TXC's on `resid_L10` (TSAE-paper
re-trained at `kval_topk=20` per `notes/tsae_paper_param_audit.md`,
fixing the original undertraining caveat). Repetition-rate plot
(judge-free) flags TXC's mag=+16 sentence-loop pathology as a discrete
mechanism, not a judge artifact.

## Caveats

- **TXC architecture mismatch**: Aniket's Stage B uses an in-task
  hill-climbed TXC at k=16/pos, T=6, window L0=96; our locked
  TXC-base = `txc_bare_antidead_t5` is k_pos-free per-component and
  T=5. The +1.574 peak Δgc must be confirmed under locked TXC-base /
  TXC-pro before it is the headline number. Aniket's TXC-H8 row is
  the closest existing analog to TXC-pro (subseq + matryoshka H8) and
  scored peak Δgc = +0.492; reproducing under `phase5b_subseq_h8` may
  shift this further.
- **Subject model**: Stage B uses `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B`, not
  `gemma-2-2b` as the C7 row in CLAUDE.md states. The hookpoint anchor
  (resid layer 10 in an 8B residual stream) is what Aniket's pipeline
  caches against; switching to gemma-2-2b is feasible (1-line yaml
  change in `experiments/ward_backtracking_txc/config.yaml`) but
  invalidates Stage A traces and the activation cache. Decision
  pending — see `agents/agent_back/decisions.md` § 1.
- **TSAE-paper architectural caveat**: `tsae` in the registry is Han's
  attention-based predicted+novel-codes TSAE, NOT a faithful Bhalla
  2026 port (Bhalla uses standard SAE + 20/80 high/low split +
  adjacent-token contrastive). Pragmatic Option A: use Han's TSAE at
  `kval_topk=20` and document the difference. Faithful Bhalla port
  (Option B) is ~1.5 days and explicitly deferred. See
  `agents/agent_back/decisions.md` § 2 + Aniket's
  `notes/tsae_paper_param_audit.md`.
- **Judge κ validation**: shipped on 20 transcripts. Cohen's κ =
  0.749 (coherence), 0.773 (backtracking), 1.000 (looping); raw
  agreement = 0.85 / 0.95 / 1.00. All three fields clear the κ ≥ 0.6
  / agreement ≥ 0.80 bar. Source:
  `origin/aniket-ward-stage-b:results/ward_backtracking_txc/judge_validation/kappa_report.md`
  (and `kappa_report.json`). Re-run with multi-grader if scope expands
  to the headline number anchoring an OpenReview claim.
- **Magnitude axis convention**: TXC's headline feature `f14621 pos0`
  was mined as a *negative-direction* backtracking feature (productive
  direction is mag<0). A symmetric x-axis penalises TXC for steering
  the "wrong way". Three options for the writeup are open at the end
  of `handoff_neurips_push.md`; awaiting Dmitry's call.
- **Calibration**: the `p95(|feature_act|)` and `L2(decoder)`
  calibration variants Aniket initially shipped both correct for a
  normalisation the B3 pipeline **already performs**
  (`normalize_to_dom_norm`). Use raw magnitudes; calibrated variants
  are saved as `*_DEPRECATED.png` only.

## Reproduction

```bash
# Adapt Aniket's Stage B pipeline into temp_bench (read-only port).
# Source path on origin/aniket-ward-stage-b:
#   experiments/ward_backtracking_txc/

# Headline pipeline (~3 h on 2× A40):
bash experiments/ward_backtracking_txc/run_headline_pipeline.sh

# TFA + MLC extension (~1.5 h):
bash experiments/ward_backtracking_txc/run_tfa_mlc_extension.sh

# Both scripts are idempotent: re-running skips cached layers / trained
# checkpoints / mined features.
```

When ported into `temp_bench`, the pipeline becomes
`experiments/c7_backtracking/run.py` and uses
`temp_bench.runner.run_cell` to write a single
`results/leaderboard.jsonl` row per (arch, seed, magnitude) cell.
The Stage A artefacts (`prompts.json`, `traces.json`,
`sentence_labels.json`, `dom_vectors.pt`) are read in-place from
HF-mirrored paths; Stage B never re-runs them.
