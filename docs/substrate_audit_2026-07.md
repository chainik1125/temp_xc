## Verdicts

1. **Behavioral corpus = R1-Distill-Llama-8B — APPROPRIATE.** The measured
   phenomena (backtracking self-excitation, hedging drift, assume-derive
   grammar) are long-CoT phenomena; only a reasoner emits them. The
   one-model-one-domain limitation is already recorded honestly in the
   measurement docs. No change.

2. **§ 5.2 backtracking: base reader vs reasoner generator — the SHARPEST
   FLAG.** The behavioral line measures R1-Distill-Llama-8B *behavior*, but
   the activation anchor reads **Llama-3.1-8B base** over the Ward traces.
   If (as is likely — base models do not emit long backtracking CoT) the
   math500 traces were generated by an R1-style reasoner, then the paper's
   anticipatory-backtracking signal is a **reader's** signal: textual
   predictability of upcoming backtracking to a base LM. The stronger and
   distinct claim — the **generator anticipates its own backtracking**
   (intention, not text statistics) — is untested. Because
   R1-Distill-Llama-8B is fine-tuned *from* Llama-3.1-8B, the generator is
   a drop-in cache swap on identical token streams. **Empirical arm
   delegated** (conversion-depth briefing, phase 3): cache both models on
   the same Ward stream, compare anticipation ceilings layer-by-layer.
   First step there: pin the actual trace provenance from
   `aniket-ward-stage-b` (read-only) before interpreting.

3. **§ 5.1 probing: gemma-2-2b-IT on fineweb-edu — MILD FLAG (convention
   straddle).** Base-on-webtext and IT-on-chat-data are each
   self-consistent; IT-on-webtext is neither, and most sparse-probing
   baselines pair base models with web corpora. Not obviously wrong
   (IT models are the deployment-relevant object) but the pairing should
   be a *stated choice*, not an accident. **Cheap check delegated**
   (stretch arm): gemma-2-2b base vs -it at L13 on the same fineweb
   stream — if probing conclusions flip, the paper needs a caveat.

4. **§ 5.3 EM: Qwen-Instruct organisms — APPROPRIATE.** EM organisms are
   by construction fine-tunes of IT models; the standard setup. No change.

5. **Layers (13, 10, 15, 24): mid-depth lore, never swept — this is the
   real gap, and it is exactly what the conversion-depth ablation
   measures.** The GPT-2 day-stride result shows conversion can complete
   by block 3 of 12; nothing guarantees L10/32 or L13/26 sits in the
   unconverted gap for the property probed there. Two consequences worth
   stating now: (a) any NEGATIVE/ambient verdict at a single layer (EM's
   shuffle_gap ≈ 0) is **depth-confounded until the g(ℓ) curve exists** —
   the property may be non-ambient earlier in the network; (b) if g(L10)
   is small for backtracking anticipation, the paper's positive window
   result is *understated*, not wrong — an early-layer TXC could be
   stronger. Either direction matters for the camera-ready story.

6. **Unit-of-time mismatch — note for the grounded real-side.** Behavioral
   measurements tick in *sentence index* (85 sentences/trace); activation
   caches tick in *tokens* (128-token windows, T ≤ 16). When the grounded
   benches (hedging, assumption, recipe) go to real activations, the
   windowing convention must bridge the two clocks (sentence-boundary
   pooling, or token-level windows spanning several sentences ⇒ much
   longer seq_len than 128). Unresolved design decision — do not let a
   future agent inherit seq_len=128 silently.

## Disposition

Items 2 + 5 (+3 as stretch) form the empirical program of the
`conversion-depth` briefing (GPU pod `runpod-c`). Items 1, 4 stand as
approved. Item 6 is a standing constraint recorded here and in the
research STATUS roadmap.

## Resolution (2026-07-23 — the conversion-depth session answered the open items)

Full evidence: `experiments/explorations/conversion_depth/RECORD.md`
(reviewed & approved).

- **Item 2 (§ 5.2 base reader vs generator) — ANSWERED: the signal is
  reader-predictability.** Provenance pinned first (the Ward traces ARE
  R1-Distill generations). Base and generator carry the anticipation
  signal near-identically (per-token |Δ| ≤ 0.007 everywhere; window
  Δ ≤ +0.02, late layers only). Camera-ready: state the claim as textual
  predictability of upcoming backtracking; the late-layer generator
  margin is the follow-up target for intention-like signal.
- **Item 3 (§ 5.1 gemma base/IT straddle) — ANSWERED: harmless.** Raw
  L13 probing ceilings equivalent (mean |Δ| 0.005 over 33 tasks; the one
  crossing is a both-models-degenerate probe artifact). Fix = state the
  choice; no results caveat needed.
- **Item 5 (single-layer depth confound) — ANSWERED, and it bit exactly
  where predicted:** backtracking g(ℓ) is a flat open plateau (L10 fine
  but not special — any residual layer works); **the EM negative IS
  depth-confounded** — g(ℓ) peaks +0.13 mid-depth with +0.097 at L15
  itself and a position-sensitive slice +0.11 at L13. Attribution
  precision (2026-07-23 correction): the PAPER's § 5.3 negative is "TXC
  variants underperform T-SAE on Wang-style steering + sparse-probe
  PR-AUC at L15" — the paper never reports a shuffle ablation. The
  shuffle_gap ≈ 0 was the REPO's internal detection experiment, and OUR
  gloss of it ("the property is ambient at L15") is what the depth sweep
  falsifies. The paper's empirical result stands untouched; its
  *explanation* changes — the TXC lost despite real window headroom
  (weak realization and/or readout mismatch), which is the strongest
  known candidate for a grounded position-aware win (TXC-tracking
  follow-up).
- Items 1, 4 stood approved; item 6 (sentence-vs-token clock) remains a
  standing constraint for the grounded real-side.
