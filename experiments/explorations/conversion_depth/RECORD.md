## § 0 — Provenance pin: who generated the Ward math500 traces?

**Verdict: PINNED. The 300 Stage A traces were generated by
`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B` (the reasoner), via vLLM greedy
decoding (temperature 0.0, max_tokens 2000, bf16), on 300 Sonnet-4.6-seeded
prompts. They were NOT generated by the base model, and they are NOT
MATH500 — the `math500` in the datasource name
`ward_backtracking_math500` is a historical misnomer.**

Evidence chain (all verifiable from this repo + its remotes):

1. **`results/c7_backtracking/stage_a/ATTRIBUTION.md`** (in-tree; ported
   2026-05-03 by agent_back from `origin/aniket-ward-stage-b @ a62175ee`,
   source path `results/ward_backtracking/`): "`traces.json` — 300
   reasoning traces from `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B`, one
   per prompt."
2. **`origin/aniket-ward-stage-b:experiments/ward_backtracking/generate_traces.py`**
   — the script that produced `traces.json` — hardcodes
   `model_name="deepseek-r1-distill-llama-8b"` in BOTH engine paths (vllm
   and transformers). Its docstring: "Run DeepSeek-R1-Distill on the 300
   seed prompts … with our own prompt list (from seed_prompts.py)
   **instead of MATH500/MMLU-Pro**" — the misnomer's origin: the venhoff
   trace-gen machinery it wraps was built for MATH500/MMLU-Pro, but the
   Ward replication swapped in its own prompt set.
3. **`origin/aniket-ward-stage-b:experiments/ward_backtracking/config.yaml`**:
   `models.base: meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B`,
   `models.reasoning: deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B`;
   `trace_gen: {engine: vllm, max_tokens: 2000, temperature: 0.0, dtype:
   bfloat16}`; `seed_prompts: {n_total: 300, per_category: 30,
   generator_model: claude-sonnet-4-6}` (10 categories: basic_logic,
   geometry, probability, arithmetic, counting, number_theory, set_theory,
   sequences, inequalities, algebra_word_problems).
4. **`origin/aniket-ward-stage-b:experiments/ward_backtracking/README.md`**
   (Aniket, 2026-04-28): Stage A pipeline lists "`generate_traces` —
   distill → traces.json"; Stage A's design is "DoM steering vector from
   **base** Llama-3.1-8B activations induces backtracking in
   **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B**" — i.e. the base model appears in
   Stage A only as an activation *reader*, never as the generator.
5. **The § 5.2 stream construction**
   (`origin/final:purified/src/temp_bench/data/nlp/ward.py`,
   `_load_corpus_ward`): each trace's `full_response` is tokenized with
   the **subject model's** tokenizer (for § 5.2: the NousResearch
   Llama-3.1-8B BASE mirror), sliced into non-overlapping 128-token
   windows (stride = seq_len = 128), each window decoded and re-encoded
   with `add_special_tokens=True` + `padding/truncation to 128` → the
   4044 × 128 stream of `llama_3_1_8b_base_l10_ward_nousmirror`.

**Consequence for interpretation (substrate-audit item 2, now
unconditional):** the paper's § 5.2 anchor reads **Llama-3.1-8B BASE**
