Why this run exists

The reviewers asked one question in three voices

  • NeurIPS submission 26867 drew one converging complaint across all three reviewers plus the meta-review: the paper never demonstrates that temporal aggregation (specifically, cross-position weight sharing) is responsible for its gains.
  • bbby: the isolating baseline (Stacked SAE) exists in App. A but is absent from Fig. 4 / Table 2; MLC — cross-layer, not temporal — wins sparse probing, and window length barely matters there.
  • 4z15 (accept): can you isolate the temporal contribution from generic crosscoder capacity?
  • EAxU (strong reject): results seem preliminary; marginal improvement over T-SAE and MLC; TXC is motivated primarily by backtracking.
  • The paper's own Limitations section concedes the root cause: we cannot yet predict in advance which real-world tasks will favor TXCs.

Diagnosis

Every real task in TempBench sits in regime 1 or 2 of the synthetic program's coordinate system: the latent is either per-token-readable (nobody separates) or linear-in-window (every window arch ties). Regime 3 — latents needing cross-position comparison — is the only regime that separates window architectures from each other, and the paper's own architecture is the unique family that can read it.

Conversion

The screening question is: will the model decline to maintain this as a per-position state? Anything generatively useful at the probe position is linearized per-position by mid-depth. Five of six round-1 candidates died here (forbidden-word onset: per-token climbs 0.538 → 0.668 with depth while the window gap never opens).

The additive-code theorem

synthetic/changepoint/bench_record.md § 3

Per-token SAE, T-SAE, Stacked and TXC-pre codes are all additive over per-position features. For any per-position functions a_t, cov(Σ_t a_t(m_t), τ) = 0 term-by-term when marginals are balanced ⇒ equality-pattern latents are invisible to every additive code, at any capacity (measured: |τ| ≤ 0.02 at d_sae=40, T=8). TXC-post — the paper's Eq. 1 — is the one family whose nonlinearity crosses positions, making it a coincidence detector (τ = 0.66, c_t = 0.90).

Design rule. Target a label that is an equality / match between positions with balanced per-position marginals. Then five of six architectures sit at a provable chance floor and only TXC-post can read it — which is simultaneously bbby's missing ablation, 4z15's isolation request, and the missing theory EAxU wants. It is also immune to the strongest hostile objection ("just mean-pool the per-token codes"): pooling is additive, so it cannot compute equality either.
