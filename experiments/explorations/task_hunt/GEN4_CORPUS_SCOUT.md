## § 2 Design facts

- **Corpora committed** with pinned revisions + funnel receipts
  (pg19 new-corpus rules): `labels/pull_wikitext103.py` →
  `wikitext103_corpus.json.gz` (400 docs, stream-order first-N,
  label-free) and `labels/pull_pycode.py` → `pycode_corpus.json.gz`
  (400 whole files, stream-order first-N, autogenerated excluded,
  permissive-licence allow-list only — copyleft filtered at pull,
  the text ships in-repo — py3-parseable, per-file licence shipped).
- **Label streams committed** for the hunt4 first-wave pair (gpt2,
  gemma2): `labels/gen4c_<corpus>_<tok>.npz` (token ids, doc offsets,
  seed-0 doc split, boundary/identifier masks, fp16 faces, bins,
  position-matched manifests). llama31 numbers are in the stats JSON;
  its npz and ALL per-T floors are regenerable deterministically by
  the committed builder (`labels/build_gen4c.py`) — committed-weight
  discipline, stated deviation from hunt4's fp16-floor bundles.
- **Per-doc token cap 1024** (uniform, all tokenizers) so every
  stream row is screenable by gpt2 (its context limit; wikitext docs
  hit 1085 uncapped — found in smoke, capped at build).
- Face logic: hunt3/hunt4 machinery imported verbatim; scout-specific
  logic in `labels/gen4c_lib.py` under `tests/test_gen4c_labels.py`
  (9 green; full suite green at the commit — one pre-existing
  `code_version` contract gap on untracked-only trees noted in the
  LOG, not touched: core is frozen to me).

  substrate-generality follow-on; if it KILLs, this scout prices
  whether the family gets a second chance on richer return
